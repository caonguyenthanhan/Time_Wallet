# Time Wallet — 15s Product Intro Storylines

> Target: ~15 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · Music + UI SFX (no voiceover)
> Brand palette grounded in `app/src/main/java/com/timewallet/mvp/ui/theme/Theme.kt`: `#F5F7F3` sage paper, `#172B27` forest ink, `#176B57` / `#103F35` / `#258F76` emerald mint, `#DCF3E8` mint soft, `#FFFFFF` cards (`#DDE8E0` stroke).
> Real assets: `Biểu tượng ứng dụng Ví Thời Gian.png`, `docs/brand/wally-hero.webp`, `docs/brand/TimeWallet_Wally_Icon_Style_Guide_v3.svg`, and `docs/screenshots/ui-preview/`.

## Option A (Recommended): "24 Honest Hours"
*Recommended because it turns the user's exact brief—a fade-in title, animated key features, and subtle background music—into a cohesive product journey using Time Wallet's real screens without gimmicks.*

- **Device & Form:** Moment in someone's day · Object-to-object chain (each UI surface morphs or opens directly into the next feature).
- **What the viewer sees & feels:** A viewer starts at an unfilled 24-hour day bar, watches the **Time Wallet** title fade in smoothly, and follows one day being captured honestly—via live Timer, Wally AI proposals, and Plan-vs-Actual Schedule—until the interactive 2D Donut fills with clarity and zero guilt. The tone is calm, grounded, and reassuring rather than frantic productivity hype.
- **First 2 seconds (exact):** Frame 0 opens on the warm `#F5F7F3` sage stage with the real `VÍ THỜI GIAN HÔM NAY` emerald hero card (`14h 50`) partially filled on the 24h bar as **"Time Wallet"** (`#172B27`) fades in cleanly from a soft 8 px blur-dissolve at center-left with the tagline `"Know where your 24 hours truly go"`.
- **Beats:** `hook (unfilled 24h bar + fade-in title) → feature 1 (live Timer + category tag morph) → feature 2 (Wally AI chat turning a sentence into confirmed proposals) → feature 3 (Plan-vs-Actual schedule diff + interactive 2D Donut pop) → payoff & end card (official wallet icon + "Time Wallet · Own your 24 hours")`.
- **Look:** Warm sage paper `#F5F7F3` ground, `#172B27` forest ink and `#176B57` emerald mint accent, Outfit (700/800) display + Inter (400/600) body, real crisp mobile UI frames (`#FFFFFF` cards with `#DDE8E0` 2 px strokes and soft `0 18px 40px` contact shadows) plus 2% subtle tactile grain.
- **Sound:** Gentle, minimal warm Rhodes/synth-pad groove (~96 BPM) entering cleanly after a crisp clock-tick opener, punctuated by subtle cursor clicks (`click`), soft UI pops (`pop`) as tags/proposals land, and a gentle fade-out under the closing mark.

## Option B: "Meet Wally, Your Time Coach"
- **Device & Form:** A character does the work · One continuous horizontal stage (the camera pans smoothly across one world as Wally guides each feature).
- **What the viewer sees & feels:** Time Wallet's mascot Wally (`wally-hero.webp`) acts as a friendly, physical timekeeper across a bright mint studio bench, bringing warmth and personality to time tracking. Viewers feel welcomed by a non-judgmental assistant who helps log activities, confirm AI suggestions, and inspect weekly balance without ever auto-writing behind their back.
- **First 2 seconds (exact):** On a bright `#F5F7F3` / `#DCF3E8` studio sweep, Wally pops cleanly into frame with his checklist clipboard as the **"Time Wallet"** wordmark fades in softly beside the official wallet icon and Wally taps the emerald `Bắt đầu đo` pill to start the clock.
- **Beats:** `hook (Wally enters + fade-in title) → feature 1 (Wally triggers live Timer ring) → feature 2 (Wally parses a natural-language day into 5 confirmed proposal cards) → feature 3 (camera glides to 7-day 106h 15 analytics + Wally AI Coach Spotlight) → end card (Wally raises his checklist beside the Time Wallet mark)`.
- **Look:** Bright mint-tinted studio `#F5F7F3` with `#DCF3E8` floor band, `#172B27` ink and `#258F76` bright emerald accent, Outfit (700) + Inter (500), transparent 3D Wally mascot (`wally-hero.webp`) paired with 2D vector UI cards and 0.45–0.65 damping tactile springs on position.
- **Sound:** Light, upbeat marimba-and-pad acoustic underscore (~108 BPM) with soft key-ticks on Wally's chat prompt, crisp pops on each checklist/proposal card, and a gentle chime-resolve as Wally lands at the end card.

## Option C (Bold): "Zero Fake Scores — The Honest Ledger"
- **Device & Form:** Life before & after + Type carries the argument · One frame that rebuilds on the musical beat.
- **What the viewer sees & feels:** Instead of gliding between phone screens, one geometric architectural grid splits, snaps, and rebuilds right in front of the viewer—contrasting vague, untracked hours with Time Wallet's strict 5-layer truth (`Measured`, `Timed`, `Duration-only`, `Active`, `Planned`). The viewer feels an immediate "aha" of mental clarity as chaos snaps into structured emerald order.
- **First 2 seconds (exact):** Scattered muted gray hour blocks (`#E0E6E0`) drift across a crisp deep-emerald `#103F35` canvas; at `0.2 s` **"Time Wallet"** fades in bold cream (`#F5F7F3`) at center while the grid snaps into a sharp 3-column ledger structure (`Measured · Timed · Unplaced`).
- **Beats:** `hook (chaotic hours + fade-in title) → reveal (ledger columns snap into focus) → feature 1 (live Timer + Quick Log "Đọc sách 30p" stays duration-only without fake timestamps) → feature 2 (Plan vs Actual "KH 1h → TT 1h 12p" + Wally AI confirm gate) → payoff & end card (2D Donut ring closes + official emerald wallet mark)`.
- **Look:** High-contrast split between deep forest `#103F35` and warm paper `#F5F7F3` grounds, `#258F76` / `#DCF3E8` accents, Archivo Black / Outfit (800) kinetic headlines + JetBrains Mono (500) tabular time readouts (`00:45`, `14h 50`, `+12p`), sharp 2 px vector grid lines and embedded real UI crops.
- **Sound:** Clean, modern minimalist pulse (~112 BPM) where the intro holds on quiet ambient texture + ticking pulse for 1.5 s, opens into a warm low-key electronic bed as the ledger snaps into alignment, and resolves onto a warm sustained chord at the end card.
